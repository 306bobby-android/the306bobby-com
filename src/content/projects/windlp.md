`yt-dlp` is excellent and entirely a command-line tool. That's a wall for anyone who
doesn't live in a terminal.

WinDLP is the smallest thing that gets over it: paste a URL, choose MP4 or MP3, and it
downloads and converts to something that will actually play. Whole-playlist downloading is
in as an experimental option.

Deliberately not a front-end for every flag `yt-dlp` has — the point is that someone who
would never open a terminal can use it.
